## Logo Concept & Meaning

### Logo History & Symbolism

* **Logotype / Name:** `GEN XVI`

* **Core Philosophy:**
  The logo's meaning aligns with the tagline **#InclusiveForInnovation**, which states that inclusivity is the root of innovation.

* **Visual Characteristics:**

  * **Folded Paper in Roman Numeral `XVI`:** Symbolizes the process of opening a new page and conveys BEM XVI's openness to diverse aspirations and limitless ideas.

  * **Inclusive Space:** Serves as a foundation where innovation can grow through an abundance of ideas born from various perspectives.

  * **Impact:** BEM XVI encourages the creation of relevant innovations, provides solutions, and has a real impact on the campus environment.

## Logo Usage Guidelines

### DO'S (Correct Usage)

* **On Dark Backgrounds:** Keep the logo light (white / light colors).

* **On Light Backgrounds:** Keep the logo dark (dark blue / navy colors).

### DONT'S (Incorrect Usage)

* **Do not** rotate the logo at any angle.

* **Do not** change the existing color of the logo.

* **Do not** crop or stretch the logo.

## Supergraphic Elements & History

The supergraphic consists of 4 core visual motifs representing the pillars of BEM UMN GEN XVI:

### 1. SOLID

* **Visual Description:** Various and consistent square shapes combined at different angles.

* **Meaning:** Symbolizes a strong, stable, and unshakeable structure even if placed differently. This aligns with the meaning of **"Solid"** which means something strong and reliable.

### 2. INNOVATIVE

* **Visual Description:** Semicircle shapes joined together to form a full geometric composition.

* **Meaning:** Each semicircle resembles a part of an idea. When put together, they complete a shape that symbolizes how larger solutions are born from smaller ideas that unite by purpose. This aligns with the meaning of **"Innovative"** that BEM UMN highlights.

### 3. COLLABORATIVE

* **Visual Description:** A combination of curved and sharp edge shapes.

* **Meaning:** Reflects **"Collaborative"** where the diversity of characters, ways of thinking, and skills are not barriers, but rather complementary elements that unite individuals at UMN to achieve a common goal.

### 4. INCLUSIVE

* **Visual Description:** Four-point sparkle/star radiating from a common center.

* **Meaning:** Each distinct element radiates from the same center, showing that different parts, when united, create balance and harmony to form a sparkle. This reflects **"Inclusive"** where diversity is embraced while working toward a shared purpose.
